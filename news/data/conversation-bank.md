# Conversation Bank — small-talk drill material

Purpose: a small, compounding toolkit that makes daily small-talk reps
achievable instead of improvised. The daily feed's skill-drill slot picks
one rep from here. Review weekly; add openers/anecdotes that actually
worked, prune ones that felt forced.

## The 3-step loop (tech for every rep)

1. **Ask** — one open question (below).
2. **Paraphrase** what they said back ("so you mean…") — this is where people feel heard.
3. **One follow-up** before switching to yourself.

That loop alone removes most awkwardness. Do not aim for impressiveness;
aim for a 30-second exchange where they spoke at least twice.

## Openers (pick one per rep, rotate)

- "What are you working on this week that you're excited about?"
- "How's your family doing lately?"
- "What's the best thing you've eaten or watched recently?"
- "What's something you've been trying to get better at?"
- "What's one thing you can't stop thinking about right now?"

## Follow-ups (the step most people skip)

- "Oh, and how did that turn out?"
- "What made you start doing that?"
- "What would you do differently if you started over?"
- "What's the hardest part about that?"
- "What does success look like for you in the next few months?"

## Personal anecdotes (your 3-seed bank — expand from real life)

> These are starter seeds. Replace with real ones from your life; a
> specific real story always beats a generic one. Keep each under 15 words
> in your head: *event → feeling → one-line lesson*.

1. Daughters (Dec 2022 / Jun 2024): "Watching them learn to say their
   first full sentences taught me that confidence comes from small wins
   they can name."
2. Building the platform: "I keep hearing 'just find good influencers' —
   but the real job is matching the right content to the right channel;
   that's why we build it this way."
3. Decision-making: "I used to collect opinions; now I collect
   tradeoffs — it's how I stopped dithering."

## Daily rep menu (low-stakes, rotate through)

- Barista / shop: one opener + one follow-up, no phone, eye contact.
- Colleague / partner: one "best thing you've eaten or watched" exchange.
- Parent community (dad-of-two meetups are gold): one anecdote + one question back.
- Client call opening: 60 seconds on something non-sales before business.
- Gym / run club: one "what are you trying to get better at" exchange.

## Weekly check (goes in the weekly review)

- How many reps did I complete this week? (target: 5+)
- Which opener/follow-up felt most natural? Keep it.
- Which one felt like acting? Swap it out.
- Add one new real anecdote to the bank this week.
