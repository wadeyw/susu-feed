### 2) EmbeddingGemma 2 (Apache 2.0 open weights) makes the matching-layer infra decision concrete: don't build on hosted proprietary embeddings
- Track: ai_change
- Marker: 💡 idea
- Why it matters to me: Willison's read of Google's EmbeddingGemma 2: for embedding workloads specifically, hosted-proprietary doesn't make sense — matching applications compute and **store** millions of vectors for later comparison, and a vendor can stop offering the model at any time, which forces a full **re-embedding tax** (recompute the whole corpus + invalidate the index). - Tradeoff / tension: Open weights shift the ops burden to us (serving, model updates, quality evals) but remove the discontinuation/repricing risk. The corpus index is the asset; the model is cheap. - Implication: Embedding choice for creator matching is an **infra decision, not a feature detail**: open weights now, version the index alongside the corpus, and log re-embedding cost so the tax is a visible ledger line instead of a surprise.
