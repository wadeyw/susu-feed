### 3) Stop Making TUIs — Thomas Ptacek's case for real GUIs in the age of agents
- Track: ai_change
- Why it matters to me: This is a structural observation about cost shifts. Coding agents have dropped the marginal cost of building a usable GUI to near zero. - Implication: If you're building anything that users interact with directly, the question is no longer "can I build it?" but "should it have a real interface, and what shape should that take?" This is a differentiation lever, not just a UX preference.
