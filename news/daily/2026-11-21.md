### 1) Token fraud is real and it's already hitting AI SaaS free trials
- Track: ai_change
- Marker: 🔥
- Why it matters to me: A PM building an AI product in public documents a flood of trial abuse — free-tier abuse for cheap inference, and organized groups *reselling* tokens. If we ever ship a free tier or metered API surface (creator-matching API, content tools), this abuse surface arrives on day one.
- Tradeoff / tension: Open free tiers win funnel volume but hand out raw inference value to resellers; hard gating (payment, verification) protects margin but kills the "try before buy" motion that lowers our sales friction. The cost of retrofitting abuse detection after launch (fraud losses + trust damage) is higher than building it in.
- Implication: Trial design is now an anti-fraud engineering problem, not a marketing one: per-identity caps, device/behavior fingerprinting, human-verification for API access, and kill-switches for reseller patterns.
- Action: Add a "trial abuse" checklist (identity limits, rate caps, detection signals, revocation) to the roadmap before any free tier ships; note the ChatPRD case as the reference.
