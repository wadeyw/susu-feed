- Track: own_business
- Why it matters to me: Stripe analyzed 1 million disputes over 16 weeks and isolated which evidence types correlate with wins. This is directly actionable if you run any business with physical or digital products — dispute win rates are a function of evidence quality, not outcome fairness.
- Tradeoff / tension: Investing in evidence infrastructure (GPS maps, delivery timestamps, usage logs) vs. absorbing dispute losses. - Implication: Dispute handling is a signal-processing problem, not a legal one. The evidence you collect and when you submit it matters more than whether you're "right." For any business, the ROI of better evidence infrastructure likely exceeds the ROI of most legal strategies.
