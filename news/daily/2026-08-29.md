- Track: better_decisions
- Why it matters to me: The timeline from "bug discussed in private" to "active exploit in the wild" has compressed from days to minutes. - Tradeoff / tension: Speed of discovery is a feature of a healthier security ecosystem, but it also means defensive postures that relied on obscurity or slow disclosure cycles are obsolete. The tradeoff is between shipping fast and assuming the security perimeter is already compromised the moment something is public.
- Implication: Your risk model for any new tool, dependency, or AI agent should assume that vulnerability discovery is near-instantaneous. This means: minimize your attack surface aggressively, prefer local/offline when possible, assume supply chain trust is eroding faster than documentation admits, and treat "we'll patch it" as a timeline measured in hours, not sprints.
