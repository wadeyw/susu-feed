### 3) PostgreSQL 19: read-your-writes consistency on replica reads (WAIT FOR LSN)
- Track: better_decisions
- Marker: 📌
- Why it matters to me: Durable mental-model update: the classic "standby reads can be stale" problem now has a first-class answer — a session on a read replica can WAIT FOR LSN up to its last write's LSN, so "read after your own write" is consistent across replicas at session scope. Bonus signal: the release slipped two months largely because of the volume of AI-reported vulnerabilities — the security pipeline of the tools we depend on is changing shape.
- Tradeoff / tension: Forcing LSN waits adds per-read latency and replica coupling (reads block if the standby lags); the escape hatch is accepting eventual consistency where staleness is tolerable (analytics) and waiting only where user-visible state matters (dashboards right after an action).
- Implication: Audit our read paths: which dashboards read from replicas right after a user write? Those are the classic "I just did X and the UI doesn't show it" bugs — a per-path policy (wait vs. accept-stale) is a one-day fix list.
- Action: watch — note it in the knowledge base; run the read-path audit next time we touch dashboards.
- Confidence: high
