## Cross-industry open idea (saas)

- **SaaS → us transfer: expand many markets in parallel, and let the *integration layer* (not channel count) be the speed source.** Stripe's 100+ fastest-growing enterprises processed their first cross-border transaction ~24x faster than baseline, because they entered ~25 countries in year 1 (6x baseline) and cut time-to-add-a-new-payment-method (one enterprise: 80% less). They also kept *fewer, more deliberate* fraud rules than the average enterprise. Transfer to our going-overseas clients: don't do sequential channel-by-channel testing; stand up a reusable channel/payment/localization layer, launch multiple markets *in parallel* off it, and deliberately constrain rule-set sprawl. The moat is the reusable layer, not the number of markets.
