### 2) Provenance is becoming a trust primitive — "can you prove this output really came from X?"
- Track: better_decisions (transfers to our attribution question, AQ1)
- Marker: 📌 watch
- Why it matters to me: Anthropic is building "FlashProve" — a way for anyone to verify a response actually came from the model it claims. - Tradeoff / tension: Provenance is only as strong as its trust anchor. In AI that anchor is "signed by the model"; in creator attribution it's "signed by the channel/platform data" — and the platform is *not* our ally. Our version inherits the hard problem of who holds the anchor.
- Implication: The attribution moat isn't "we measured more"; it's a *verifiable provenance primitive* for outcomes. Start sketching a signed, auditable "this happened on this channel via this creator" ledger.

- Confidence: medium (real and early; strong transfer, but the trust-anchor problem is genuinely hard)
- Source: TechCrunch — https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/03/anthropic-is-working-on-a-system-to-prove-ai-models-identity/
