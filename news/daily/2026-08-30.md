### 2) Domain-Driven Agents: LLMs fail on legacy code because of missing language, not missing capability
- Track: remembering
- Why it matters to me: This is a concrete framework for when AI agents actually help vs. when they make things worse. The key insight — from coldtake.dev — is that LLMs work fine on greenfield projects but quality drops sharply in legacy codebases with heavy dependencies. The root cause isn't the model; it's missing a shared language (ubiquitous language from DDD). - Tradeoff / tension: The strategic layer (context map, glossaries, bounded contexts) still requires human judgment and can't be automated away. Skip the strategic layer and agents will accelerate mess.
- Implication: For personal knowledge systems, this means writing CONTEXT.md for any project before handing it to an agent.
