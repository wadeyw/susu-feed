- Track: family_future
- Marker: 📌
- Why it matters to me: A new public benchmark (arXiv, 09-23) collected 175,000+ student-AI messages and measured GRE learning gains: AI and human tutoring produced equivalent gains. - Tradeoff / tension: The equivalence is on standardized knowledge gain (GRE quant, adult/teen cohort). - Implication: Design principle for their schooling years: AI as the knowledge channel, humans as the confidence/motivation channel — and keep a "question log" (what did they ask today, why) as the earliest self-directed-learning dataset we'll ever have on our own kids.
