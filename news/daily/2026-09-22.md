### 1) Jev "System One" / decision models: an LLM shape built to *score*, not talk
- Track: ai_change
- Marker: 💡 idea
- Why it matters to me: TypeSafe's Jev is called a new model category — "System One" or **decision models**. Unlike a text LLM, it takes text in and returns **structured numeric outputs** (category weights, yes/no, ratings, with confidence scores). This is exactly the shape needed for a machine-evaluable creator-match score rather than a free-text "here are 5 suggestions you'll have to read."
- Tradeoff / tension: A scoring model gives you a crisp, comparable number, but it can't explain *why* — you still need a language layer for the human rationale and edge cases. And it's a 2026 early product, not battle-tested: treat the architecture idea, not the vendor.
- Implication: Concretizes AQ2's "embed a matching engine." Two-layer pattern: (1) a decision/scoring model that ranks creator↔brand fit as structured scores with confidence, (2) a language model that turns the top scores into a human-defensible brief. The scoring layer is what makes "result-driven" measurable instead of vibes — a defensible moat.
- Action: Think — spec a minimal "creator-fit scorer": inputs (creator category, audience geo, past content-perf), outputs (fit score + top-3 reasoning fields). Test on one live client match to check whether structured scores actually beat our current heuristics.
- Confidence: medium (novel shape, fresh, but early product)
